The first public release of Eagle Customizer, a lightweight app written in pure Rust for setting up your mouse, keyboard, audio devices and touchpad.

### Highlights
- **Mouse:** remap up to 12 buttons to any of 52 built-in actions, and set pointer speed, acceleration, scrolling and left-handed mode
- **Keyboard:** remap any key on a live keyboard picture, and set key-repeat delay and speed
- **Audio:** volume, mute, default device and a test sound for headphones, speakers and microphones
- **Touchpad:** pointer speed, tap to click, natural and two-finger scrolling, and disable while typing
- **Theme:** follows the system light/dark theme and accent color
- **Runs light:** remapping keeps working after you close the window, and the engine uses only about 3 MB of RAM
- **Safe by design:** a locked-down system service with no network access, and your key presses never leave the engine
